## 1. Research purpose

Identify mature or semi-mature precedents relevant to reconstructing Pingyao Zhenguo Temple Wanfo Hall as a complete 3D digital timber-architecture model from measured-survey reports, drawings, photographs, historical materials, and related evidence.

The research question was not how to finish the existing implementation, but how mature practices have approached this class of Chinese timber reconstruction problem.

## 4. Initial cross-case findings

Within the P01–P24 discovery range:

- Chinese timber digital-reconstruction precedents do exist.
- Several cases suggest that a global datum, grid, or structural organization is established before detailed component work; this was not yet a Wanfo Hall rule.
- Point-cloud or surface geometry alone is insufficient to establish hidden timber connections.
- “Whole-building model” has different meanings across cases.
- A complete HBIM does not by itself prove deterministic regeneration.
- Component-level parametric success does not prove whole-building scaling.
- No single case identified in the initial P01–P24 pool demonstrated all Wanfo Hall goals simultaneously.
