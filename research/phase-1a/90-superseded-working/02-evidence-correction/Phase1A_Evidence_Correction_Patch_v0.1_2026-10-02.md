## 1. Purpose

Correct the initial Phase 1A evidence baseline without expanding the candidate pool or entering route selection. The patch focused on bibliographic recovery, evidence-level discipline, validation distinctions, and identification of unresolved sources.

## 2. Evidence levels introduced

- FACT-FULLTEXT — original full text checked and the claim falls within its support.
- FACT-ABSTRACT — original publisher/author abstract directly supports the claim.
- FACT-METADATA — only bibliographic/existence metadata confirmed.
- INFERENCE — synthesis from confirmed facts, not a direct source conclusion.
- WORKING HYPOTHESIS — worth testing but not proven.
- UNKNOWN — insufficient evidence.

## 4. Corrected evidence judgments

- complete 3D model ≠ deterministic regeneration;
- mortise geometry ≠ topology / connection semantics;
- measured current condition ≠ ideal or rule-based reconstruction;
- component-level parametric generation ≠ proven whole-building scaling;
- a construction sequence or virtual construction animation ≠ physical mortise-tenon assembly validation.
