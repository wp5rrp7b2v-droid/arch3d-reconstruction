# T-008 P2.3 Known Limitations and Evidence Boundaries V001

This deliverable is a **controlled 963 reconstruction candidate**. It is not a complete restoration of the building's 963 appearance and makes no claim that every modeled member is an original 963 component.

1. `Z-006` remains `UNKNOWN / null / DO_NOT_LOCK`. The modeled column height comes only from D-023's replaceable `Z-006-RC-01 = 11 × MOD-006`; the resolved 3534.3 mm is not a confirmed historical height.
2. `DG-114` does not establish a unified small-dou specification. The `BRACKET_CONTACT` blocks are graphic symbols scaled from the approved diagrammatic module section. Their fraction is a versioned component-library rule, not a measured historical size.
3. Bracket jumps follow the approved two-jump and four-jump aggregate controls. Individual jump subdivisions, block placement, joint form and 45° corner carpentry are schematic. Mortises, tenons and the hidden-angle beam dispute are unresolved.
4. The six roof-envelope strips are zero-thickness planar interpolation between approved eave-to-ridge controls. They do not specify individual tiles, historic roof build-up, curvature, precise gable-edge construction, or weathering.
5. `FRAME_SUPPORT` instances are explicit bounded diagrammatic connectors copied from approved P2.2 frame-tier/post endpoints. Their P2.2 controls remain separately diagnostic. They do not certify exact historic member sections, joints, or locations beyond the approved medium-LOD topology.
6. Perimeter walls, doors, windows, decoration, individual tilework and small fittings have not been filled in without approved evidence. Open elevations and gable ends in review views are deliberate evidence boundaries, not claims that the historic hall had no enclosure.
7. `HIS-002` remains a component-level originality constraint. All 365 modeled instances have `originality_status=unknown`; no object is labeled a confirmed 963 original.
8. Formal `observed_as_measured`, `report_ideal_model` and `reconstructed_963_candidate` values remain separate. P2.3 instances carry the candidate state and retain their source parameter layers; observed measurements were not copied into candidate geometry.
9. The evidence diagnostic uses system colors for broad evidence/placeholder categories. It is an engineering review aid; the per-instance manifest and metadata remain the authoritative explanation of a component's source and status.

Product Owner visual review and a separate P2.3 Gate decision remain required.
